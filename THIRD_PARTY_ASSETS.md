# Third-Party Asset Registry

Every imported asset MUST be recorded here **before** it is committed
(spec §8). Preserve original licenses and attribution files alongside the
asset under `assets/imported/<source>/`.

## Notes

- FlightGear aircraft/scenery assets are typically **GPL-2.0+**; acceptable
  for this local, non-distributed project — still record them here.
- Do not import: Boeing manuals/diagrams, airline documents, commercial
  simulator assets, or any material without a clear license. Keep personal
  reference copies under `private/` (gitignored).
