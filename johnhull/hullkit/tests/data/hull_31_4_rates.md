# Hull §31.4 original rate observations

Source: author VasicekCIR.xls, sheet `Data (See Section 31.4)`, recovered from the [official Rotman FinHub archive](https://github.com/rotmanfinhub/john-hull-textbook-resources/blob/9b8dbfe37661dbd3de65d3a1489dc1297e840de7/Options%2C%20Futures%2C%20and%20Other%20Derivatives%2C%2011th%20Edition/Vasicek%20and%20CIR%20worksheets/VasicekCIR.xls). Snapshot checked 2026-10-04. Original cells exported to date/percent CSV; no fitted values included. 8664 observations, 1982-01-04 through 2016-08-23. Divide percent by 100. The book uses 1/250 year per adjacent observation, including calendar gaps; the observations are a three-month rate proxy for the instantaneous short rate. External series identifier, rate basis and market-quote construction are not specified; do not label this DTB3/DGS3MO or replace it with a current download.

Table31.1 uses the 2016-08-23 market snapshot and r0=0.003, nine equally weighted continuous zero yields. Book p727 prints 0.168 in the Q-level formula although the preceding P-level is 0.0168; canonical computation follows the P/Q algebra and the original worksheet. Auxiliary Problem31_16.xls has EXP(gamma*T-1) in its B denominator instead of EXP(gamma*T)-1 inside the product; its saved fit is separate from canonical CIR mathematics. Neither discrepancy is presented as an author-confirmed erratum.
