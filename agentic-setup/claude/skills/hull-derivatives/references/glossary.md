# Glossary

Paraphrased one-line definitions for Hull 11e glossary terms. For longer treatments, follow the link to the topic or chapter file.

> **Source**: Hull 11e end-of-book glossary (pp. 827-850). Definitions are paraphrased for personal use.

---

## A

- **ABS**: See Asset-Backed Security.
- **ABS CDO**: Structured instrument whose tranches are formed from the tranches of asset-backed securities. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.8.
- **Accrual Swap**: Interest rate swap where one side accrues interest only when a specified condition holds. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.7.
- **Accrued Interest**: Interest accumulated on a bond since the most recent coupon payment. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Adaptive Mesh Model**: Numerical tree model (Figlewski and Gao) that uses a fine grid in critical price regions and a coarse grid elsewhere. → [topics/numerical_methods.md](topics/numerical_methods.md), Ch.21.
- **Agency Costs**: Expenses that arise when an agent's incentives diverge from the principal's interests. → Ch.16.
- **Agency Mortgage-Backed Security**: MBS whose payments carry a government-agency guarantee. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.8.
- **American Option**: Option that may be exercised at any point up to and including its expiration date. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.10.
- **Amortizing Swap**: Swap in which the notional principal decreases on a predetermined schedule over time. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.7.
- **Analytic Result**: A closed-form expression (equation) for a derivative's value or property. → [topics/bsm.md](topics/bsm.md), Ch.15.
- **Arbitrage**: Trading strategy that earns a riskless profit from zero net initial investment by exploiting mispricing between two or more securities. → Ch.1, Ch.5.
- **Arbitrageur**: Trader who pursues arbitrage opportunities. → Ch.1.
- **Asian Option**: Option whose payoff depends on the average price of the underlying over some period. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Ask Price**: Price at which a dealer offers to sell an asset (also called Asked Price or Offer Price). → Ch.1.
- **Asked Price**: See Ask Price.
- **Asset-Backed Security (ABS)**: Security backed by a pool of loans, bonds, credit-card receivables, or similar assets. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.8.
- **Asset-or-Nothing Call Option**: Binary option paying the asset price when the asset finishes above the strike, zero otherwise. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Asset-or-Nothing Put Option**: Binary option paying the asset price when the asset finishes below the strike, zero otherwise. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Asset Swap**: Swap exchanging a bond's fixed coupon for LIBOR plus a spread. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.7.
- **As-You-Like-It Option**: See Chooser Option.
- **At-the-Money Option**: Option whose strike price equals the current price of the underlying asset. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.10.
- **Average Price Call Option**: Option paying the greater of zero and the amount by which the average asset price exceeds the strike. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Average Price Put Option**: Option paying the greater of zero and the amount by which the strike exceeds the average asset price. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Average Strike Option**: Option whose payoff equals the difference between the final asset price and the average price over the option's life. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.

## B

- **Bachelier Normal Model**: Interest rate model in which a future rate is assumed to follow a normal distribution. → [topics/ir_derivatives.md](topics/ir_derivatives.md), Ch.29.
- **Backdating**: Practice (often illegal) of recording a document with a date earlier than the actual date. → Ch.16.
- **Back Testing**: Testing a risk model (e.g., VaR) using historical market data to assess its accuracy. → [topics/risk_management.md](topics/risk_management.md), Ch.22.
- **Backward Induction**: Procedure for valuing a derivative by working from the terminal nodes of a tree back to the present. → [topics/binomial.md](topics/binomial.md), Ch.13.
- **Barrier Option**: Option whose existence or payoff depends on whether the underlying price touches a predetermined barrier level. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Base Correlation**: Correlation that, under the Gaussian copula, makes a 0%-to-X% CDO tranche price consistent with the market for a given X. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Basel Committee**: International regulatory body overseeing bank capital and risk standards. → [topics/risk_management.md](topics/risk_management.md), Ch.22.
- **Basis**: Spot price minus futures price (or vice versa, depending on convention) for a commodity or financial asset. → [topics/hedging.md](topics/hedging.md), Ch.3.
- **Basis Point**: One hundredth of one percent ($= 0.01\%$) used to quote interest rate changes. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Basis Risk**: Risk that the basis (spot minus futures) changes unexpectedly, reducing hedge effectiveness. → [topics/hedging.md](topics/hedging.md), Ch.3.
- **Basis Swap**: Swap exchanging cash flows tied to one floating reference rate for cash flows tied to another floating rate. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.7.
- **Basket Credit Default Swap**: Credit default swap referencing a basket of several entities rather than a single one. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Basket Option**: Option whose payoff depends on the aggregate value of a portfolio of assets. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Bear Spread**: Options strategy profiting from a price decline; typically a short lower-strike put combined with a long higher-strike put (or equivalent calls). → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.
- **Bermudan Option**: Option exercisable only on a discrete set of specified dates during its life. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.10.
- **Beta**: Measure of an asset's systematic (market) risk relative to the overall market. → [topics/hedging.md](topics/hedging.md), Ch.3.
- **Bid-Ask Spread**: Difference between the dealer's ask price and bid price. → Ch.1.
- **Bid-Offer Spread**: See Bid-Ask Spread.
- **Bid Price**: Price at which a dealer is willing to buy an asset. → Ch.1.
- **Bilateral Clearing**: Two-party arrangement for managing OTC derivative transactions, often under an ISDA Master Agreement. → Ch.2.
- **Binary Credit Default Swap**: Credit default swap paying a fixed dollar amount upon default by the reference entity. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Binary Option**: Option with a discontinuous payoff, such as cash-or-nothing or asset-or-nothing. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Binomial Model**: Discrete-time asset-price model allowing only two possible price movements in each short interval. → [topics/binomial.md](topics/binomial.md), Ch.13.
- **Binomial Tree**: Lattice diagram showing how asset price evolves under the binomial model. → [topics/binomial.md](topics/binomial.md), Ch.13.
- **Bivariate Normal Distribution**: Joint distribution of two correlated variables each of which is normally distributed. → [topics/stochastic_calculus.md](topics/stochastic_calculus.md), Ch.14.
- **Black's Approximation**: Fischer Black's method for approximating the value of a call option on a dividend-paying stock. → [topics/bsm.md](topics/bsm.md), Ch.15.
- **Black's Model**: Extension of Black-Scholes-Merton for European options on futures; widely used when the asset price at maturity is assumed lognormal. → [topics/ir_derivatives.md](topics/ir_derivatives.md), Ch.18, Ch.29.
- **Black-Scholes-Merton Model**: Continuous-time model for pricing European options on a lognormal underlying, developed by Black, Scholes, and Merton. → [topics/bsm.md](topics/bsm.md), Ch.15.
- **Bond Option**: Option in which the underlying asset is a bond. → [topics/ir_derivatives.md](topics/ir_derivatives.md), Ch.29.
- **Bond Yield**: Discount rate that equates a bond's present value of cash flows to its market price. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Bootstrap Method**: Procedure for constructing zero or forward rates sequentially from market instruments of increasing maturity. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Boston Option**: See Deferred Payment Option.
- **Box Spread**: Combination of a bull call spread and a bear put spread with the same strikes. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.
- **Break Forward**: See Deferred Payment Option.
- **Brownian Motion**: See Wiener Process.
- **Bull Spread**: Options strategy profiting from a price rise; typically a long lower-strike call and short higher-strike call (or equivalent puts). → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.
- **Butterfly Spread**: Options position combining two long calls (at extreme strikes $K_1$ and $K_3$) and two short calls (at middle strike $K_2$) where $K_2 = 0.5(K_1+K_3)$. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.

## C

- **Calendar Spread**: Options position that is long a call expiring at one date and short a call expiring at a different date, same strike. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.
- **Calibration**: Process of inferring model parameters from observed market prices of actively traded instruments. → [topics/numerical_methods.md](topics/numerical_methods.md), Ch.27.
- **Callable Bond**: Bond with a provision allowing the issuer to repurchase it at a set price before maturity. → [topics/ir_derivatives.md](topics/ir_derivatives.md), Ch.29.
- **Call Option**: Contract giving the holder the right to buy an asset at a predetermined price by a specified date. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.10.
- **Cancelable Swap**: Swap that one counterparty may terminate early on prespecified dates. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.7.
- **Cap**: See Interest Rate Cap.
- **Cap Rate**: The strike rate above which payoffs are triggered in an interest rate cap. → [topics/ir_derivatives.md](topics/ir_derivatives.md), Ch.29.
- **Capital Asset Pricing Model (CAPM)**: Model linking an asset's expected return to its beta (systematic risk). → Ch.3.
- **Capital Valuation Adjustment (KVA)**: Valuation adjustment capturing the cost of holding regulatory capital against derivatives exposures. → Ch.9.
- **Caplet**: A single component period of an interest rate cap. → [topics/ir_derivatives.md](topics/ir_derivatives.md), Ch.29.
- **Case-Shiller Index**: US house-price index. → Ch.35.
- **Cash Flow Mapping**: Technique for expressing an instrument as a portfolio of zero-coupon bonds to facilitate VaR calculation. → [topics/risk_management.md](topics/risk_management.md), Ch.22.
- **Cash-or-Nothing Call Option**: Binary call paying a fixed amount if the asset finishes above the strike, zero otherwise. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Cash-or-Nothing Put Option**: Binary put paying a fixed amount if the asset finishes below the strike, zero otherwise. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Cash Settlement**: Settling a futures or option contract in cash rather than delivering the underlying asset. → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.2.
- **CAT Bond**: Catastrophe bond whose interest or principal is reduced if insured losses from a specified event exceed a threshold. → Ch.35.
- **CCP**: See Central Counterparty.
- **CDD**: Cooling degree days — maximum of zero and the daily average temperature above 65°F, used in weather derivatives. → [topics/commodity_energy.md](topics/commodity_energy.md), Ch.35.
- **CDO**: See Collateralized Debt Obligation.
- **CDO Squared**: Instrument in which default risks from a portfolio of CDO tranches are reallocated into new securities. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **CDS**: See Credit Default Swap.
- **CDS Spread**: Annual basis-point fee paid by protection buyer in a credit default swap. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **CDX NA IG**: Index referencing a portfolio of 125 North American investment-grade companies. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **CEBO**: See Credit Event Binary Option.
- **Central Clearing**: Use of a central counterparty (CCP) to clear OTC derivative transactions. → Ch.2.
- **Central Counterparty (CCP)**: Clearinghouse that interposes itself between counterparties in OTC derivatives to guarantee performance. → Ch.2.
- **Cheapest-to-Deliver Bond**: The bond most economical to deliver into the CME Group Treasury bond futures contract. → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.6.
- **Cholesky Decomposition**: Matrix factorization technique used to sample correlated variables from a multivariate normal distribution. → [topics/risk_management.md](topics/risk_management.md), Ch.22.
- **Chooser Option**: Option allowing the holder to decide at a specified date whether it is a call or a put. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Class of Options**: See Option Class.
- **Clean Price of Bond**: Quoted bond price excluding accrued interest (dirty price = clean price + accrued interest). → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Clearing House**: Firm that guarantees the obligations of both parties to a derivatives transaction. → Ch.2.
- **Clearing Margin**: Margin posted by a clearing member at the clearinghouse. → Ch.2.
- **Cliquet Option**: Series of at-the-money options with strike prices reset at each expiry; each option begins when the previous ends. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **CMO**: Collateralized Mortgage Obligation.
- **Collar**: See Interest Rate Collar.
- **Collateral**: Cash or securities posted by a party to protect its counterparty against default. → Ch.2.
- **Collateralization**: System requiring one or both counterparties to post collateral against outstanding derivatives positions. → Ch.2.
- **Collateralized Debt Obligation (CDO)**: Structured security in which credit risk from a bond portfolio is repackaged into tranches with varying seniority. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Collateralized Mortgage Obligation (CMO)**: MBS in which investors are divided into classes receiving interest and principal repayments according to specified rules. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.8.
- **Combination**: Options position using both calls and puts on the same underlying. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.
- **Commodity Futures Trading Commission (CFTC)**: US regulator for futures and swaps markets. → Ch.2.
- **Commodity Swap**: Swap where cash flows depend on the price of a commodity. → [topics/commodity_energy.md](topics/commodity_energy.md), Ch.35.
- **Compound Correlation**: Correlation implied from the market price of a specific CDO tranche. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Compound Option**: An option whose underlying is itself another option. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Compounding Frequency**: Convention specifying how often interest is compounded when quoting a rate. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Compounding Swap**: Swap in which interest compounds rather than being paid periodically. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.34.
- **Compression**: Multilateral process for reducing gross notional and capital requirements by netting and canceling offsetting trades. → Ch.2.
- **Conditional Value at Risk (C-VaR)**: See Expected Shortfall.
- **Confirmation**: Written contract formalizing a verbal OTC derivatives trade. → Ch.7.
- **Constant Elasticity of Variance (CEV) Model**: Model where the variance of a short-period price change is proportional to the asset price level. → [topics/numerical_methods.md](topics/numerical_methods.md), Ch.27.
- **Constant Maturity Swap (CMS)**: Swap exchanging a swap rate for either a fixed rate or a floating rate on each payment date. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.34.
- **Constant Maturity Treasury Swap**: Swap exchanging a Treasury bond yield for a fixed or floating rate on each payment date. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.34.
- **Consumption Asset**: Asset held primarily for consumption rather than investment (e.g., crude oil, copper). → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.5.
- **Contango**: Situation where the futures price exceeds the expected future spot price (or simply the current spot price). → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.5.
- **Continuous Compounding**: Interest-rate quoting convention equivalent to the limit as the compounding interval shrinks to zero. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Control Variate Technique**: Variance-reduction method for numerical procedures that uses a related analytically solvable problem to improve accuracy. → [topics/numerical_methods.md](topics/numerical_methods.md), Ch.21.
- **Convenience Yield**: Benefit accruing to the physical holder of a commodity that is not available to a holder of a futures contract. → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.5.
- **Conversion Factor**: Scaling factor used to determine the number of bonds deliverable in a CME Treasury bond futures contract. → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.6.
- **Convertible Bond**: Corporate bond that can be exchanged for a fixed number of equity shares at the holder's discretion. → Ch.27.
- **Convexity**: Measure of the curvature in the bond-price–yield relationship, capturing second-order price sensitivity. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Convexity Adjustment**: Correction needed to convert a futures rate to a forward rate, or a forward rate when Black's model is used. → [topics/ir_derivatives.md](topics/ir_derivatives.md), Ch.30.
- **Copula**: Function defining the correlation structure between variables with known marginal distributions. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Cornish-Fisher Expansion**: Approximation relating the quantiles of a distribution to its moments. → [topics/risk_management.md](topics/risk_management.md), Ch.22.
- **Cost of Carry**: Net cost of holding an asset — storage costs plus financing cost minus income earned. → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.5.
- **Counterparty**: The other side in a financial transaction. → Ch.1.
- **Coupon**: Periodic interest payment made on a bond. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Covariance**: Measure of the linear co-movement between two variables (equals their correlation times the product of their standard deviations). → [topics/risk_management.md](topics/risk_management.md), Ch.22.
- **Covariance Matrix**: See Variance-Covariance Matrix.
- **Covered Call**: Position consisting of a short call option combined with a long position in the underlying asset. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.
- **Crashophobia**: Market-observed tendency to price deep out-of-the-money puts at elevated implied volatilities, attributed to fear of a crash. → [topics/vol_smile_surface.md](topics/vol_smile_surface.md), Ch.20.
- **Credit Contagion**: The tendency for a default by one firm to trigger defaults at other firms. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.24.
- **Credit Default Swap (CDS)**: Contract giving the buyer the right to sell a bond at face value upon default of the issuer. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Credit Derivative**: Derivative whose payoff is contingent on the credit quality of one or more reference entities. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Credit Event**: Occurrence (e.g., default or restructuring) that triggers payment under a credit derivative. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Credit Event Binary Option (CEBO)**: Exchange-traded option paying a fixed amount if a reference entity suffers a credit event. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Credit Index**: Index tracking protection costs for a portfolio of reference entities (e.g., CDX NA IG, iTraxx Europe). → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Credit Rating**: Measure of the creditworthiness of a bond or issuer. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.24.
- **Credit Ratings Transition Matrix**: Table showing the probability that a bond migrates from one rating to another over a given period. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.24.
- **Credit Risk**: Risk of loss due to a counterparty defaulting on its obligations. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.24.
- **Credit Spread Option**: Option whose payoff depends on the spread between yields on two assets. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.25.
- **Credit Support Annex (CSA)**: Part of the ISDA Master Agreement specifying collateral posting requirements. → Ch.9.
- **Credit Valuation Adjustment (CVA)**: Adjustment to the fair value of a derivative to account for the possibility of counterparty default. → Ch.9.
- **Credit Value at Risk**: Credit loss that will not be exceeded at a given confidence level. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.24.
- **CreditMetrics**: Industry methodology for computing credit value at risk. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.24.
- **Cross Hedging**: Hedging exposure to one asset using a futures or forward contract on a different but correlated asset. → [topics/hedging.md](topics/hedging.md), Ch.3.
- **Cumulative Distribution Function**: Probability that a random variable falls below a given value $x$. → [topics/stochastic_calculus.md](topics/stochastic_calculus.md), Ch.14.
- **Currency Swap**: Swap exchanging principal and interest in one currency for principal and interest in another currency. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.7.
- **CVA**: See Credit Valuation Adjustment.

## D

- **Day Count**: Convention governing how interest accrues between dates when quoting rates. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Day Trade**: Trade opened and closed within the same trading day. → Ch.2.
- **Debt (or Debit) Valuation Adjustment (DVA)**: Value to a company arising from the possibility that it might itself default on its derivatives obligations. → Ch.9.
- **Default Correlation**: Measure of the tendency of two companies to default at approximately the same time. → [topics/credit.md](topics/credit.md), Ch.24.
- **Default Intensity**: See Hazard Rate.
- **Deferred Swap**: Agreement to enter into a swap at a future date (also called a forward swap). → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.7.
- **Delivery Price**: Price agreed upon in a forward contract for delivery of the underlying asset. → [topics/futures_forwards.md](topics/futures_forwards.md), Ch.1.
- **Delta ($\Delta$)**: Rate of change of a derivative's price with respect to the price of the underlying asset. → [topics/greeks.md](topics/greeks.md), Ch.19.
- **Delta Hedging**: Hedging strategy that makes a derivatives portfolio insensitive to small moves in the underlying by maintaining a delta-neutral position. → [topics/greeks.md](topics/greeks.md), Ch.19.
- **Delta-Neutral Portfolio**: Portfolio with delta equal to zero, hence locally insensitive to small underlying price changes. → [topics/greeks.md](topics/greeks.md), Ch.19.
- **DerivaGem**: Option-pricing software accompanying Hull's textbook. → Ch.13.
- **Derivative**: Financial instrument whose value is derived from the price of another underlying asset. → Ch.1.
- **Deterministic Variable**: Variable whose future value is known with certainty. → [topics/stochastic_calculus.md](topics/stochastic_calculus.md), Ch.14.
- **Diagonal Spread**: Options position with both different strike prices and different maturities. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.12.
- **Differential Swap**: Swap where a floating rate in one currency is applied to a principal in a different currency. → [topics/swaps.md](topics/swaps.md), Ch.30.
- **Diffusion Process**: Stochastic process for an asset price that evolves continuously without jumps. → [topics/stochastic_calculus.md](topics/stochastic_calculus.md), Ch.14.
- **Dirty Price of Bond**: Full cash price of a bond including accrued interest. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Discount Bond**: See Zero-Coupon Bond.
- **Discount Instrument**: Non-coupon-bearing instrument such as a Treasury bill. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Diversification**: Risk-reduction strategy of spreading a portfolio across many different assets. → [topics/risk_management.md](topics/risk_management.md), Ch.22.
- **Dividend**: Cash payment distributed to shareholders from a stock's earnings. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.15.
- **Dividend Yield**: Dividend expressed as a percentage of the stock price. → [topics/bsm.md](topics/bsm.md), Ch.15.
- **Dodd-Frank Act**: US 2010 legislation to reform financial regulation, including OTC derivatives markets. → Ch.2.
- **Dollar Duration**: Product of a bond's modified duration and its price, measuring price sensitivity in dollar terms. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **DOOM Option**: Deep-out-of-the-money put option. → [topics/vol_smile_surface.md](topics/vol_smile_surface.md), Ch.20.
- **Down-and-In Option**: Barrier option that comes into existence only if the underlying price falls to a preset barrier. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Down-and-Out Option**: Barrier option that ceases to exist if the underlying price falls to a preset barrier. → [topics/exotics.md](topics/exotics.md), Ch.26.
- **Downgrade Trigger**: Contractual clause requiring additional collateral or early termination if a party's credit rating falls below a threshold. → Ch.9.
- **Drift Rate**: Expected rate of change per unit time in a stochastic variable. → [topics/stochastic_calculus.md](topics/stochastic_calculus.md), Ch.14.
- **Duration**: Weighted-average life of a bond's cash flows; also approximates the proportional price change for a given yield change. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Duration Matching**: Asset-liability management technique that equates the durations of assets and liabilities. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **DV01**: Dollar value of a 1-basis-point upward parallel shift in all interest rates. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **DVA**: See Debt (or Debit) Valuation Adjustment.
- **Dynamic Hedging**: Hedging by continuously or periodically rebalancing the underlying position to maintain a target (usually delta-neutral) exposure. → [topics/greeks.md](topics/greeks.md), Ch.19.

## E

- **Early Exercise**: Exercise of an option prior to its maturity date. → [topics/options_basics.md](topics/options_basics.md), Ch.11.
- **Effective Federal Funds Rate**: Weighted average rate on brokered overnight federal funds transactions. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Efficient Market Hypothesis**: Hypothesis stating that asset prices fully and immediately reflect all relevant information. → Ch.1.

## Z

- **Zero-Coupon Bond**: Bond making no coupon payments; purchased at a discount and redeemed at par. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Zero-Coupon Interest Rate**: Interest rate that would be earned on a bond paying no coupons; also called spot rate or zero rate. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Zero-Coupon Yield Curve**: Plot of zero-coupon interest rates against time to maturity. → [topics/interest_rates.md](topics/interest_rates.md), Ch.4.
- **Zero Curve**: See Zero-Coupon Yield Curve.
- **Zero Rate**: See Zero-Coupon Interest Rate.
