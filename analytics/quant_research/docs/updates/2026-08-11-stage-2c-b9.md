- sourceはM6 panel、previous-filing provenance、hash-verified normalized document、
  pre-analysis contract
- inner train 192行、inner validation 64行
- `target_available_date < 2023-10-23`かつcompany development rule
- target accessionとlocked outer rowを含まない
- raw text、normalized text、CIK、accessionを含まない
